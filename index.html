Parameter set description

Sequence notation: Ae, Ce, Ge and Ue represent 2′-MOE-A, 2′-MOE-C, 2′-MOE-G and 2′-MOE-U, respectively. DNA residues are written as dA, dC, dG and dT.

RRHD: one strand is fully 2′-MOE modified and hybridized with RNA. ALT: one strand contains partial/intermittent 2′-MOE modifications and is hybridized with RNA. The 2′-MOE-specific RRHD and ALT nearest-neighbor parameters used in this calculator were determined in the present work.

Gapmer: terminal wings are 2′-MOE modified and the central gap is DNA. Local regions are assigned to RRHD, RNA/DNA, or ALT-based junction parameters.

Non-crowding condition: 100 mM NaCl, 10 mM Na2HPO4 (pH 7.0 at 37 °C), and 1 mM Na2EDTA. Cell-like condition: the same buffer containing 40 wt% PEG200.

Self-complementary correction is applied only when Strand A is identical to its own reverse-complement sequence.

Parameter version: 17 September 2026.
